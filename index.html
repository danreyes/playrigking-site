How it plays

One trailer. Too many cars.

Every second you ride the flatbed counts. Everyone else wants your spot, and the shed-built kit on your roof is there to help them lose theirs.

1

Catch the rig

Line up behind the moving truck and drive up its narrow wheel-track ramps. Miss the line and you're back in the dirt.

2

Hold the deck

Your time only counts while you're aboard. Hold your line through the bends, bumps and ferry crossings.

3

Knock them off

Roof rockets, an oil spill and a spare tyre fired out of the boot. The bus has one enormous Grand Finale.

Couch play

Your phone is the controller.

Play on the big screen with an Apple TV, iPad or Mac. Scan the QR code on the TV and your phone becomes a steering wheel with pedals, or plug in any game controller.

Two players, split screen

Side by side on one TV, both against the AI rivals. Each player picks any car the TV owns.

No accounts, no ads

No sign-up and no tracking. Buy once and it's yours on every Apple device in the family.
