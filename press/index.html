About the game

A truck hauls a thin flatbed trailer round the track. Everyone else is trying to drive up its two narrow ramps and stay aboard, and the car with the most time on the deck wins. Roof rockets, an oil spill and a spare tyre fired from the boot keep the deck contested. Tracks run from a country dirt speedway to a sea-cliff bridge, a crocodile river with a ferry crossing, and a road train in the outback. On the big screen, phones become steering wheels.
