Credits

Most of what you see in Rig King was made for it. These tools and artists helped with the rest.

Made for Rig King

Every plant, tree, building and sign in the game was modelled for Rig King, and so was every vehicle you can drive and every animal:

  • more than 60 kinds of tree and plant, mostly Australian natives, from river red gums, coolibahs and boabs to tree ferns, palms, spinifex and kangaroo paw;
  • the saltwater crocodiles, red kangaroos, wallabies, emus, cassowaries, Brahman cattle and birds;
  • the rig and its trailer, the road train, the cars, the bus, and the sedans, utes, 4WDs and caravans parked at Dusty Creek and Long Haul, and the hatchbacks, old utes, panel vans and dual-cabs parked along Nightcap Drive's village street;
  • the Croc River ferry;
  • the water: the river and creek, the sea, the waterfall pools and the billabong, drawn by our own water shader;
  • the buildings, from the Dusty Creek grandstand and the Long Ways Roadhouse to the Nightcap village and the farm sheds, windmills and tanks;
  • the road signs, event props, cliffs and termite mounds.
