Privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026.

The short version

  • Rig King has no accounts of its own, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. The game sends no data to us.
  • If you sign in to Game Center (iPhone, iPad and Apple TV) or Google Play Games (Android), your race scores go to Apple or Google for the leaderboards. If you don't sign in, nothing is sent.
  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • Your settings, unlocked tracks and personal bests stay on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

The game stores your settings (such as controls, graphics and sound), which tracks and vehicles you've chosen, your training progress, the tracks you've unlocked, your personal bests and what you've purchased, so it can work offline. Leaderboard scores waiting to be sent (for example, after racing offline) are also kept on your device until they go. The game also keeps short performance logs (frame rate, memory and device temperature per race) to help us improve it. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Leaderboards (Game Center and Google Play Games)

Rig King has online leaderboards for each track and car. They are run by Apple's Game Center on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, and by Google Play Games on Android. They are optional: the game uses them only if you're signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games on your device.

When you finish a race, the game sends two scores to Apple or Google: your total time aboard the trailer and your longest single stint, with the track and car you raced. They appear on the leaderboards with your Game Center or Google Play Games nickname, where other players can see them. Two-player races and practice don't send scores.

The game itself receives only what it needs from Game Center or Google Play Games to show you the leaderboards, such as your nickname. It does not receive your Apple Account or Google Account email address, and it sends nothing to us. Apple and Google handle this information under their own privacy policies. To stop sending scores, sign out of Game Center or Google Play Games in your device's settings; your personal bests still stay on your device.

Phones as controllers (local network)

If you use a phone as a controller, the game on your TV, iPad or Mac and the game on your phone talk to each other directly over your local Wi-Fi network. They share your phone's device name (shown on the TV so players know who's who), the controls you press, and the state of your car. None of this leaves your home network or reaches us. That's why your device asks for permission to find and connect to devices on your local network.

Camera

If you scan the TV's join code from inside the app, the camera is used only to read that code, on your device. Images are not stored or sent anywhere. You can also scan the code with your phone's own Camera app instead.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We receive confirmation of what was bought so the game can unlock it, but never your name, payment details or billing address.

Crash reports

The game itself sends no crash reports or diagnostics. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. We use these only to fix problems.

Children

Rig King doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Whether a child can use Game Center or Google Play Games, and which leaderboards and nicknames they see, is controlled by the parental settings of Apple's Screen Time and Google's Family Link.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
