Privacy policy

Draft for review. Effective date: to be set at launch.

Draft: prepared from how the game works today. Please review it (and, if you like, have it checked) before publishing.

The short version

  • Rig King has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • Your settings and game progress stay on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

The game stores your settings (such as controls, graphics and sound), which tracks and vehicles you've chosen, your training progress and what you've purchased, so it can work offline. It also keeps short performance logs (frame rate, memory and device temperature per race) to help us improve the game. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Phones as controllers (local network)

If you use a phone as a controller, the game on your TV, iPad or Mac and the game on your phone talk to each other directly over your local Wi-Fi network. They share your phone's device name (shown on the TV so players know who's who), the controls you press, and the state of your car. None of this leaves your home network or reaches us. That's why your device asks for permission to find and connect to devices on your local network.

Camera

If you scan the TV's join code from inside the app, the camera is used only to read that code, on your device. Images are not stored or sent anywhere. You can also scan the code with your phone's own Camera app instead.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive confirmation of what was bought so the game can unlock it, but never your name, payment details or billing address.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share diagnostics with Apple or Google, they may pass anonymous crash reports to developers. We use these only to fix problems.

Children

Rig King doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
